MULTI-DEVICE SOFTWARE MODEL
- Separate jobs keep separate target UUID/attachment/hash/chip/expected identity.
- One review approves the frozen plan; target/configuration/package changes invalidate it.
- All packages are validated before first writing; each job revalidates its own target.
- Stop-after-current finishes the current job and preserves queued jobs.
- A failed job stops subsequent writes and leaves an unconfirmed result/history.
- Process death during a job restores it as unconfirmed, without automatic write resume.
- A reused USB address/MIDI ID, duplicate or null serial does not merge physical records.
- Lost sibling evidence stays invalid even if that sibling returns at the old address.
- Multiple return candidates/port pairs cannot be selected by an override.
- Stale nonce/USB epoch/MIDI-generation approvals cannot authorize a new attachment.

PHYSICAL ACCEPTANCE — REAL PHONES AND AT LEAST TWO FM-1 UNITS
Use Android 14 and 16 devices and the intended powered/passive hubs. Identify the
units physically before assigning names. Keep instrument backups where possible.
- Record normal/OTA/final USB descriptors, port counts, serial null/duplicates,
  enumeration order, grant behavior and elapsed reconnect times. Keep raw serials local.
- Assign different compatible packages to two units; review the target/hash mapping.
- Verify one active mode-changing job and that no neighbor receives upgrade/data commands.
- Verify both planned returns, expected loader identity and final model/version.
- With multiple units, verify the inline pairing path, no extra ordinary modal approval,
  and remaining system USB grants. Single-device automatic matching has stricter rules.
- Confirm hub/socket changes are not presented as persistent hardware identity.
- Deny a grant, detach during the grant dialog and rotate while it is pending;
  retry explicitly and ensure no unintended transfer starts.
- Background the activity or turn off the screen during a controlled normal transfer;
  return through the ongoing notification. Verify inline pairing when required.
- Verify stop-after-current, per-job history and readable failure reasons.
- Exercise unexpected sibling/hub detach in controlled NON-WRITING phases; ensure
  continuity loss blocks further writes. Do not intentionally unplug an active flash write.
- After an interrupted/unconfirmed attempt, inspect hardware before any new write.
  Re-read/re-pair devices and rebuild affected jobs; never resume from a saved percentage.
- Do not infer stable power merely from successful enumeration or a progress indicator.

SEPARATE EXPERIMENTAL ACCEPTANCE
- Logger buffers are bounded; connection/flashing stops monitoring automatically.
- JEDEC detection reports a separate ROM unit and never assigns its result to a MIDI card.
- Recovery is opt-in, requires the recognized/hash-pinned V15 plan and verified backup,
  excludes bootloader writes and has no automatic fallback from OTA.
- Test ROM recovery with appropriate physical equipment separately from OTA acceptance.

OUT OF SCOPE FOR ANDROID RESULTS
No physical hub success is implied by the deterministic tests. No Xcode/native iOS
binary or iOS fleet parity is established; perform that platform's acceptance separately.
