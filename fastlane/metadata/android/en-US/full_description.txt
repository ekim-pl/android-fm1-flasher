Browse a firmware catalog and prepare USB MIDI updates for your M-VAVE FM-1 from an Android device.

Firmware library
• Explore categories, search by firmware name or author, and sort releases.
• Keep favorites on your device without an account.
• Download supported packages, manage your library and check for catalog updates.
• Use verified, previously downloaded packages offline.

Choose your instrument
• Explicitly select one USB MIDI device, including when several devices share a hub.
• Reconnect automatically after restarts with a single USB device and one MIDI port; otherwise confirm the new port manually.
• Check package SHA-256, UFW structure, relevant CRCs and catalog compatibility before writing.
• Keep a progress notification during the firmware update.

Make it yours
Choose English, Polish or Simplified Chinese, or follow the system language. Use light or dark appearance, or let Android choose.

Requirements
Android 8.0 or later, USB Host/OTG and Android MIDI support, an M-VAVE FM-1, a data cable and stable power. A powered USB hub is recommended. Internet is needed to obtain the catalog and new packages. Firmware descriptions remain in the authors’ original language.

Experimental software
This version has not yet been verified with a physical FM-1. Firmware updates can erase instrument data. Wrong packages, loss of power or selecting the wrong instrument after a restart can require hardware recovery. Back up instrument data with the firmware’s own tools before use. This app does not provide backups, Bluetooth updates, automatic flash-chip detection or hardware recovery.
