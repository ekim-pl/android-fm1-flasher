connectedDevice foreground service — implemented 0.9.0 use case
Draft factual description; not a submitted store declaration or an existing video.

The owner reads FM-1 instruments, assigns locally downloaded compatible firmware,
and approves the complete plan in Tasks. FlasherService executes those immutable
jobs sequentially, with one mode-changing session at a time. It never redirects a
failed job's package to another instrument. ROM recovery uses a separate explicit plan.

The foreground notification reports stage/progress and returns to ComposeActivity.
A time-limited wake lock supports the active transfer when another app is foregrounded
or the screen is off. Neither mechanism guarantees USB power or immunity to process
termination. A pairing/system permission action can require returning to the app.
The foreground operation ends on completion/failure; stop-after-current preserves
remaining jobs. After process death a running job is unconfirmed, not automatically resumed.
Background catalog checks use WorkManager, not the flash foreground operation.

Single-device automatic reconnect requires one eligible MIDI return and one USB
device with no MIDI siblings. Multiple devices need a unique return, uninterrupted
neighbors and inline owner pairing. Hub/neighbor loss or ambiguity blocks further
writing. Android USB permission may be required after re-enumeration.
