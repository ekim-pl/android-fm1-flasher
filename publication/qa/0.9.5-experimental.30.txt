CI FOLLOW-UP
The initial CI run exposed a readiness race in the deferred-export instrumentation
test: the Activity binds separately and can trigger a new foreground refresh after
the test observes the service as idle. The test now waits for the Activity's binding
and checks readiness/enables diagnostics/delivers the result in one main-thread turn.
It still asserts a busy refresh and actual exported event contents. Both operation-log
tests passed locally on Android 16 after this test-only correction. The APK is unchanged.
